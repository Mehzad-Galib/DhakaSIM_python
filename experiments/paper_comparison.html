Reading the statistics

The t-test and K-S p-values compare per-link values pooled across seeds, which is the unit the paper's own figures plot. Links within one run share a traffic stream and are not independent, so those p-values are optimistic — read them as an indication of effect size, not as proof. The error bars on the charts are the standard deviation across seeds, which is the honest replication unit, and they rest on 3 seeds.

What this does not test

The paper's Table IV validates simulated travel times against observed ones collected from Google Maps on real Dhaka routes (Shankar–Palashi), reporting ME, MAE, RMSE, MAPE and RMSPE plus t-test and K-S p-values, and concluding 9 of 12 cases match at the 5% level with a mean MAPE accuracy of 88%. Reproducing that needs the field data, which this project does not have. experiments/stats.py implements every one of those measures, so the comparison becomes a single command once observed travel times exist.

The paper's Miami and Riyadh comparisons (its Figs. 13 and 14) are also out of scope here: this sweep covers Dhaka's topology only.
